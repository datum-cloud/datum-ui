Tidier `AppNavigation` sections and spacing.

- `group` headers line up with the item icons below them, get space above, and hide in the icon rail, which now shows each item's icon instead of one button per section.
- Items under a `group` render as top-level rows, so they match the height of other top-level rows.
- Rows sit further from the sidebar edge, and nested rows line up under their parent's label.
